How to use ScriptCutter

ScriptCutter watches your raw video, finds the parts where you said a line again, and removes the old tries and the long pauses. You always get a new timeline. Your original video is never changed.

Before your first video (only once)

  1. Open DaVinci Resolve.
  2. Go to DaVinci Resolve → Settings → System → General.
  3. Set External scripting using to Local, click Save, and restart Resolve. (Studio only. The free version doesn't need this.)

Cut a video in 6 steps (DaVinci Resolve)

  1. In Resolve, put your raw recording on video track 1 of a timeline.
  2. Open Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter. This page opens.
  3. Choose Talking head (you talking to the camera) or Screen recording (a tutorial on your screen).
  4. Press Analyse and wait. A 5-minute video takes less than a minute.
  5. Check the list. Click Brown at the top to see only the parts that need your eyes. Press ▶ to listen, then click Keep or Cut.
  6. At the bottom, choose Export as: DaVinci Resolve and press Create in Resolve. Your new, cleaned timeline opens in Resolve, with overlapping cuts and Voice Isolation.

Get the finished video, or edit in another app

  1. Choose Video file and drag your raw recording into the box (or use your Resolve timeline).
  2. Press Analyse and check the list, just like above.
  3. In the bar at the bottom, pick what you want under Export as: MP4 video (the finished cut, ready to upload), DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or another editor. Then press the button next to it. Files are saved next to your video.
  4. Premiere Pro: File → Import, pick the .xml file. Final Cut Pro: File → Import → XML, pick the .fcpxml file. Other apps: import the .edl file.

The MP4 has straight cuts and keeps the brown parts. In Premiere and Final Cut the cut opens on one video track, and brown parts have a marker with the reason. Overlapping A/B cuts and Voice Isolation only work in DaVinci Resolve.

Hindi letters or Hinglish?

For Hindi videos, the switch above the list shows the words in हिंदी letters, in Hinglish (Hindi in English letters, like a chat), or as an English translation. ScriptCutter compares retakes through the English translation and uses the Hindi words to find restarts inside long sentences.

Transcript of the final cut

Only when you want it: press Transcript in the bottom bar to read what is said in your finished cut, line by line with the time. Copy it, or download it as subtitles (.srt, works on YouTube and in every editor), as text (.txt) or as a PDF.

Have a script? Paste it

If you recorded from a script, paste it in Step 4 before pressing Analyse. Hindi letters, Hinglish or English all work, even mixed. ScriptCutter then finds every line in your recording, keeps your last good take of each line and cuts the other takes and anything that is not in the script. Longer parts that are not in the script stay brown, in case they are a good ad-lib. Above the list you see how many lines were found, and which ones were not.

The steps at the top

Each step of the work shows at the top of the list: ✓ is done, ⚑ means it needs your eyes (click it to see those parts). More steps, like captions and B-roll images, will appear here as ScriptCutter grows.

Free or Studio version of Resolve?

  • Studio: everything above happens by itself.
  • Free: the free version only allows scripts started from its own menu, so there are two small extra clicks. Always open this page from Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter (do it again after you switch to another timeline). After you press Create in Resolve, go back to Resolve and choose Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter once more. It builds the timeline.
  • AI Voice Isolation only works in Studio. Everything else works in both.

What the colours mean

Blue: kept. ScriptCutter is sure this is a good take.
Brown: kept, but please check it. It might be a mistake or a repeat.
Grey, crossed out: removed. Usually an older try of a line you said again.

In Resolve, brown clips have a marker with the reason. Press Shift + ↓ to jump to the next one.

Record so it works best

  • When you make a mistake, pause for a second and then say the whole sentence again from the start.
  • Your last try is the one that is kept, so make the last one your best one.
  • Saying "let me do that again" or "cut" before a retake helps it find the mistake.
  • Record in a quiet room. Background music makes speech harder to understand.

Useful buttons

  • Cautious / Balanced / Aggressive: how sure it must be before cutting. Not happy? Switch it. The list updates at once.
  • All brown → Keep / Cut: decide all brown parts with one click.
  • Pace & padding: give your words more or less breathing room.
  • Keyboard: ↑ ↓ move, Space play, 1 keep, 2 check later, 3 cut, ? opens this help.

It gets smarter with you

When you change Keep or Cut and then create the timeline, ScriptCutter remembers your choice. After a few videos it cuts the way you like.

Something is not working?

  • "Resolve not reachable": open a project in Resolve and check the setting from the first section.
  • ScriptCutter is missing from the menu: run the installer again and restart Resolve.
  • "Can't see your Resolve timeline": open this page from Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter in Resolve.
  • The video does not play here: click ▶ Audio only.
  • Too many brown parts: choose Aggressive, or install a bigger speech model by running the installer again.
