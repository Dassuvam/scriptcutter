How to use ScriptCutter

ScriptCutter watches your raw video, finds the parts where you said a line again, and removes the old tries and the long pauses. You always get a new timeline. Your original video is never changed.

Before your first video (only once)

  1. Open DaVinci Resolve.
  2. Go to DaVinci Resolve → Settings → System → General.
  3. Set External scripting using to Local, click Save, and restart Resolve. (Studio only. The free version doesn't need this.)

Cut a video in 6 steps

  1. In Resolve, put your raw recording on video track 1 of a timeline.
  2. Open Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter. This page opens.
  3. Choose Talking head (you talking to the camera) or Screen recording (a tutorial on your screen).
  4. Press Analyse and wait. A 5-minute video takes less than a minute.
  5. Check the list. Click Brown at the top to see only the parts that need your eyes. Press ▶ to listen, then click Keep or Cut.
  6. Press Create timeline in Resolve. Your new, cleaned timeline opens in Resolve.

Free or Studio version of Resolve?

  • Studio: everything above happens by itself.
  • Free: the free version only allows scripts started from its own menu, so there are two small extra clicks. Always open this page from Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter (do it again after you switch to another timeline). After you press Create timeline in Resolve, go back to Resolve and choose Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter - Build Timeline.
  • AI Voice Isolation only works in Studio. Everything else works in both.

What the colours mean

Blue: kept. ScriptCutter is sure this is a good take.
Brown: kept, but please check it. It might be a mistake or a repeat.
Grey, crossed out: removed. Usually an older try of a line you said again.

In Resolve, brown clips have a marker with the reason. Press Shift + ↓ to jump to the next one.

Record so it works best

  • When you make a mistake, pause for a second and then say the whole sentence again from the start.
  • Your last try is the one that is kept, so make the last one your best one.
  • Saying "let me do that again" or "cut" before a retake helps it find the mistake.
  • Record in a quiet room. Background music makes speech harder to understand.

Useful buttons

  • Cautious / Balanced / Aggressive: how sure it must be before cutting. Not happy? Switch it. The list updates at once.
  • All brown → Keep / Cut: decide all brown parts with one click.
  • Pace & padding: give your words more or less breathing room.
  • Keyboard: ↑ ↓ move, Space play, 1 keep, 2 check later, 3 cut, ? opens this help.

It gets smarter with you

When you change Keep or Cut and then create the timeline, ScriptCutter remembers your choice. After a few videos it cuts the way you like.

Something is not working?

  • "Resolve not reachable": open a project in Resolve and check the setting from the first section.
  • ScriptCutter is missing from the menu: run the installer again and restart Resolve.
  • "Can't see your Resolve timeline": open this page from Workspace → Scripts → ScriptCutter in Resolve.
  • The video does not play here: click ▶ Audio only.
  • Too many brown parts: choose Aggressive, or install a bigger speech model by running the installer again.
